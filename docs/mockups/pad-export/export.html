Export from the world menu

The pictures come from the app. The menus, the forms, and the sheets on top of them are HTML. The base is the Atlas workspace of the iPad layout at 1194 by 834 points. The flow is: the world menu, then the export form with the options, then the share sheet of the system.

1. Now. The three export rows are at the bottom of the Pages panel, below the paper color and the layout grid. You open the panel with a tap on the page outside the frames, and then you scroll.

2. The world menu. The folder button opens the world menu. "Export…" is the last row, after a divider, with the share icon. The row shows in the Atlas workspace. Nothing on the screen changes until you open the menu.

3. The export form, PDF, all pages. "Export…" opens a form sheet in the middle of the screen. The strip at the top shows the pages that go in the file. A tap on a page adds it or removes it, and Pages follows. A PDF has no options. Its raster layers have the resolution of each page. "Export…" at the top right makes the file and then opens the share sheet.

Desktop

9. Desktop. File › Export… with ⌘E opens a sheet of the window with the same options. "Export…" in the sheet opens the save panel of macOS. The tool bar has no export button. The window is schematic, because the app does not have the desktop layout yet.
